🎯 My Mission

At Appalachian IT Solutions, my mission is simple: to empower individuals, families, and local small businesses with dependable, high-quality technology and cybersecurity solutions at an economical price. Reliable tech shouldn't be a luxury reserved only for large corporations. I am dedicated to providing accessible, honest, and expert technical consulting that protects what matters most without breaking the bank.

🎓 Background & Technical Expertise

With over 5+ years of dedicated professional experience in IT administration, network engineering, and cybersecurity, I bring hands-on expertise to every deployment. I hold an Associate of Science degree in Computer Science / Systems Management from Northeast State Community College and am actively completing my Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity through Western Governors University (WGU).

I hold multiple industry certifications (including CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+, and CSIS) and maintain official vendor partnerships with top security platforms and Ubiquiti UniFi networking hardware to deliver enterprise-grade defense.
