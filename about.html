About Me & My Mission

Reliable, Economical & Enterprise-Grade IT Solutions for Homes & Local Businesses

Cheyenne Cody Barnes

Owner & Technology Consultant

🎯 My Mission

At Appalachian IT Solutions, my mission is simple: to empower individuals, families, and local small businesses with dependable, high-quality technology and cybersecurity solutions at an economical price. Reliable technology shouldn't be a luxury reserved only for giant corporations. I am dedicated to bridging that gap by providing accessible, honest, and expert technical consulting that protects what matters most without breaking the bank.

🎓 Background & Technical Expertise

With over 5+ years of dedicated professional experience in IT administration, network engineering, and cybersecurity, I bring hands-on expertise to every deployment. I hold an Associate of Science degree in Computer Science / Systems Management from Northeast State Community College and am actively completing my Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity through Western Governors University (WGU).

To ensure my clients receive industry-leading defense and infrastructure, I hold multiple professional certifications (including CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+, and CSIS) and maintain official vendor partnerships with top cybersecurity platforms and **Ubiquiti UniFi** networking hardware.

🛡️ Digital Safety for Youth & Families

I am deeply committed to protecting youth and supporting families against online threats, cyberbullying, and inappropriate content. Through custom-configured network shields, DNS filtering, and parental access controls, I help parents build safe digital enclaves at home so children can explore, learn, and play online securely.

🐾 Animal Rescue & Crisis Response

In my personal time, I actively volunteer in animal rescue and emergency disaster relief. During the devastating Kentucky floods caused by levee breaches, as well as in the aftermath of Hurricane Helene, I mobilized to assist with animal recovery and pet crisis rescue. At home, I've rescued and trained 3 dogs and 3 cats, working continuously to ensure they all live together harmoniously as one family.

🍽️ Community & Local Support

Outside of technology, I enjoy attending church with my wife, going on cruises, and hosting a local food podcast alongside a dear friend. Our podcast is dedicated to highlighting mom-and-pop restaurants across the region, bringing an influx of new customers and visibility to small local businesses that form the heart of our community.
