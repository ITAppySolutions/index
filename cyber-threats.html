What Can Viruses, Malware, or Ransomware Do To Affect My Business?

Small businesses are the number one target for modern cybercriminals. When malicious software infiltrates your network, the consequences go far beyond a slow computer. Here is what is truly at risk:

🛑 Complete Operational Downtime

When ransomware locks down your systems, your business halts. You can't process payroll, ring up customers, access inventory, or communicate with clients. Every hour your systems are locked is directly lost revenue.

💸 Financial Theft & Extortion

Keyloggers and banking trojans silently record your keystrokes, capturing passwords to your business bank accounts and merchant portals. Ransomware attackers will outright demand thousands of dollars in cryptocurrency just to hand your files back.

📁 Catastrophic Data Loss

Without properly isolated backups, a malware infection can permanently destroy your tax documents, client lists, invoices, and employee records. Recovering this information from scratch can take months of administrative work.

📉 Reputation & Client Trust Damage

If your system is compromised and leaks your clients' personal data (or sends spam/malware emails from your business address), the hardest thing to recover isn't your files—it's your reputation. Clients trust you to keep their information safe.
